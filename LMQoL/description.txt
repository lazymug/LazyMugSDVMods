[size=6][b]LM QoL[/b][/size]

[i]A collection of small quality-of-life improvements — each one toggleable.[/i]

[b]LM QoL[/b] bundles a handful of little conveniences that smooth out the rough edges of daily farm life. Every feature can be turned on or off independently, so you only run what you actually want.

[line]

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Auto Gate[/b] — fences with gates open automatically as you approach and close behind you, so you never get stuck fumbling with them.
[*][b]Magnet Radius Foraging[/b] — nearby items are gently pulled toward you for easy pickup. Adjustable radius and pull speed.
[*][b]Sell Price Tooltip[/b] — see an item's sell value (and its best processed value) right in the tooltip. Reads the game's machine data, so machines added by mods such as [i]Cornucopia[/i] and [i]Wildflour's Atelier Goods[/i] are included automatically. Prices include your profession bonuses and quality, and the most profitable option is highlighted.
[*][b]Quick Stack to Nearby Chests[/b] [i](New in 1.3.0)[/i] — press a key to top up matching item stacks in nearby chests straight from your inventory. It only fills stacks a chest already has (nothing gets misplaced) and never touches mini-shipping bins.
[*][b]Silo Capacity[/b] [i](New in 1.4.0)[/i] — choose how much hay each silo holds instead of the usual 240. Applies to silos you already built.
[*][b]Species Tooltip[/b] [i](New in 1.4.0)[/i] — hover a crop, bush or tree to see exactly what it is, plus whether it's growing, ready to harvest, tapped or mossy. Crops, trees and bushes can each be turned on or off on their own.
[*][b]Build With Item Bags[/b] [i](New in 1.5.0)[/i] — Robin also counts building materials stored inside your Item Bags, and spends them automatically. Only active if you have the [b]Item Bags[/b] mod installed; ignored otherwise.
[*][b]Custom Charcoal Kiln[/b] [i](New in 1.6.0)[/i] — the charcoal kiln accepts driftwood, torches, wood and hardwood, each with its own yield and burn time. Previously a separate mod ([i][PFM] Custom Charcoal Kiln[/i]); now built in, and no longer needs Producer Framework Mod.
[*][b]Item Totals in Bags[/b] [i](New in 1.6.0)[/i] — with an Item Bag open, hovering an item shows how many you own in total: carried, stored in chests across the whole farm, and inside your other bags. Requires the [b]Item Bags[/b] mod.
[*][b]Sell Anything[/b] [i](New in 1.7.0)[/i] — furniture, wallpaper and big craftables can finally go in the shipping bin, and shops can optionally be allowed to buy anything. Tools, the scythe and quest items stay protected.
[/list]

[line]

[size=5][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
Every feature and its settings — including the Quick Stack key and search radius — can be adjusted in-game via [b]Generic Mod Config Menu[/b].

[line]

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Generic Mod Config Menu (optional, for in-game settings)
[*]Item Bags (optional — enables the Build With Item Bags feature)
[/list]

[line]

[size=5][b]Changelog[/b][/size]

[b]1.8.1[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Fix:[/b] processing prices now come from the game's own sell-price calculation, so they reflect every profession bonus that applies to each product — Artisan, Tiller, Rancher, Fisher/Angler, Tapper, Blacksmith, Gemologist — plus quality and the difficulty modifier. Previously a flat +40% was applied to everything whenever you had Artisan, which overstated products Artisan doesn't affect (smoked fish, syrup, bars, gems) and ignored the other professions entirely. The "Show Artisan Prices" option is now "Apply Profession Bonuses".
[/list]

[b]1.8.0[/b]
[list]
[*]Sell Price Tooltip now reads the game's own machine data instead of only knowing the vanilla machines, so processing options from [b]Cornucopia[/b], [b]Wildflour's Atelier Goods[/b] and any other mod that registers machines normally show up too — and stay correct when those mods rebalance their products.
[*]Options are listed most valuable first and capped (6 by default, configurable) so the tooltip stays readable when many machines accept the same item; extras are summarised as "+N more".
[*]Cornucopia and Wildflour's Atelier Goods are detected on startup and each gets its own switch in the settings, shown only when that mod is actually installed.
[/list]

[b]1.7.0[/b]
[list]
[*]New: [b]Sell Anything[/b] — items the game refuses to sell (furniture, wallpaper, big craftables, hats, boots…) can now be put in the shipping bin. A separate option also lets shops buy anything, rather than only their usual categories; it is off by default. Items that can't be trashed — tools, the scythe, special and quest items — remain unsellable, so nothing essential can be shipped away by mistake. Do note that weapons and rings become shippable, so take the usual care with the shipping bin.
[/list]

[b]1.6.0[/b]
[list]
[*]New: [b]Custom Charcoal Kiln[/b] — absorbs the standalone [i][PFM] Custom Charcoal Kiln[/i] mod. Driftwood, torches, wood and hardwood can all be burned for coal, each with its own yield and time. Rebuilt on vanilla machine data, so Producer Framework Mod is no longer required. If you have the old content pack installed, remove it to avoid two mods editing the same kiln.
[*]New: [b]Item Totals in Bags[/b] — hovering an item inside an open Item Bag shows how many you own in total, broken down by carried / chests / bags. Chest and bag counting can each be turned off.
[*]Silo capacity can now be raised up to 48,000 (was 4,800).
[/list]

[b]1.5.1[/b]
[list]
[*]Species Tooltip can now be toggled per kind — crops, trees and bushes have their own switches, on top of the existing master toggle.
[/list]

[b]1.5.0[/b]
[list]
[*]New: [b]Build With Item Bags[/b] — when building with Robin, materials kept inside Item Bags now count towards the requirements and are spent along with your inventory. The ingredient list stops showing them in red, too. Requires the Item Bags mod; without it nothing changes.
[/list]

[b]1.4.0[/b]
[list]
[*]New: [b]Silo Capacity[/b] — set how much hay each silo stores (vanilla is 240). Existing silos pick up the new capacity too.
[*]New: [b]Species Tooltip[/b] — hovering a crop, bush or tree names the species and shows its state (growing, ready, dead, stump, tapped, mossy, fruit waiting). Works on the canopy of grown trees, not just the trunk tile.
[/list]

[b]1.3.1[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Fix:[/b] Magnet Foraging no longer drags in items your inventory can't take. Those items used to get stuck to you, endlessly animating without ever being picked up; now they stay on the ground until you have room, exactly like vanilla.
[*][b]Fix:[/b] drops that break into several pieces (wood, stone, ore) are now pulled as a whole. Previously only the first piece followed you, so the group's average position stayed out of pickup range and the game collected none of them — leaving items circling you until something else happened to trigger the pickup.
[/list]

[b]1.3.0[/b]
[list]
[*]Added [b]Quick Stack to Nearby Chests[/b] — a configurable key that tops up existing stacks in nearby chests (mini-shipping bins are safely ignored).
[*]Added Generic Mod Config Menu options for Quick Stack (toggle, keybind, search radius).
[/list]
