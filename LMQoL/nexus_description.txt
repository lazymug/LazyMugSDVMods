[size=5][b]LM QoL[/b][/size]
A growing collection of small quality-of-life improvements for Stardew Valley, bundled into a single mod. Every feature is independent and can be switched off, so you only run what you actually want.

[line]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]

[size=3][b]Auto Gate[/b][/size]
Gates open automatically when you walk near them and close after you move away.
[list]
[*]Opens any gate within 1 tile of the player
[*]Closes automatically after a configurable delay (default: ~1 second)
[*]No more fumbling with right-click while carrying items
[/list]

[size=3][b]Magnet Radius Foraging[/b][/size]
Nearby items are pulled toward you instead of making you walk over each one.
[list]
[*]Configurable radius (1–15 tiles) and pull speed
[*]Also collects spawned forage from the ground
[*]Items your inventory can't take are left alone, so nothing ends up stuck to you
[/list]

[size=3][b]Sell Price Tooltip[/b][/size]
Hover a raw item to see what it's worth after processing, and which machine pays best.
[list]
[*]Reads the game's own machine data, so [b]modded machines are included automatically[/b] — [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/24842]Cornucopia[/url] and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/27049]Wildflour's Atelier Goods[/url] each get their own toggle when installed
[*]Prices come from the game's sell-price calculation, so they include [b]every profession bonus that applies to that product[/b] — Artisan, Tiller, Rancher, Fisher/Angler, Tapper, Blacksmith, Gemologist — plus quality
[*]Sorted from most to least profitable, with the best option marked
[*]Capped at a configurable number of lines so the tooltip stays readable
[/list]

[b]Example tooltip:[/b]
[code]
--- Processing ---
Keg: 1050g (Starfruit Wine) *
Preserves Jar: 400g (Starfruit Jelly)
Dehydrator: 315g (Dried Starfruit)
(+2 more)
* = best profit
[/code]

[size=3][b]Quick Stack to Nearby Chests[/b][/size]
One key to tidy your inventory into the chests around you.
[list]
[*]Only tops up stacks a chest already has, so nothing gets misplaced
[*]Configurable key and search radius
[*]Mini-shipping bins are never touched
[/list]

[size=3][b]Species Tooltip[/b][/size]
Hover a crop, bush or tree to see exactly what it is.
[list]
[*]Names the species and shows its state: growing (with stage), ready to harvest, dead, stump, tapped, mossy, fruit waiting
[*]Works on the canopy of a grown tree, not just the trunk tile
[*]Crops, trees and bushes can each be turned on or off separately
[/list]

[size=3][b]Sell Anything[/b][/size]
Sell the things the game normally refuses to take.
[list]
[*]Furniture, wallpaper, big craftables, hats and boots can go in the shipping bin
[*]Optional setting to let shops buy anything, not just their usual categories (off by default)
[*]Tools, the scythe, special and quest items stay protected, so nothing essential can be shipped away by mistake
[/list]

[size=3][b]Silo Capacity[/b][/size]
Decide how much hay a silo holds.
[list]
[*]Anywhere from the vanilla 240 up to 48,000 per silo
[*]Applies to silos you already built, not just new ones
[*]Off by default — your save is untouched until you turn it on
[/list]

[size=3][b]Custom Charcoal Kiln[/b][/size]
The charcoal kiln stops being a wood-only machine.
[list]
[*]Driftwood → 1 coal (40m) · Torch → 3 coal (30m) · Wood → 5 coal (60m) · Hardwood → 45 coal (550m)
[*]Replaces the vanilla 10-wood recipe
[*]This was previously the separate [i][PFM] Custom Charcoal Kiln[/i] mod; it is now built in and [b]no longer needs Producer Framework Mod[/b]. If you still have that content pack installed, remove it so the two don't collide.
[/list]

[size=3][b]Item Bags integration[/b][/size]
Two features that only wake up when [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5382]Item Bags[/url] is installed.
[list]
[*][b]Build With Item Bags[/b] — Robin counts building materials stored inside your bags and spends them automatically, instead of showing them in red as if you had none
[*][b]Item Totals in Bags[/b] — with a bag open, hovering an item shows how many you own in total: carried, in chests across the whole farm, and inside your other bags
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
Everything is configurable in-game through [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5098]Generic Mod Config Menu[/url] — each feature has its own section, and options for optional integrations only appear when the relevant mod is installed.

Settings are also available in [b]config.json[/b] if you prefer editing by hand.

[line]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [url=https://smapi.io]SMAPI[/url]
[*]Install [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5098]Generic Mod Config Menu[/url] (optional, for in-game settings)
[*]Download this mod and extract the [b]LMQoL[/b] folder into your [b]Mods[/b] directory
[*]Launch the game through SMAPI
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Stardew Valley 1.6+ · SMAPI 4.0+
[*]Single-player and multiplayer
[*]Most features write nothing into your save and can be added or removed freely
[*]One exception: [b]Silo Capacity[/b] stores the new capacity on the silos themselves. If you remove the mod (or turn the option off) while a silo holds more than 240 hay, the surplus stays in the silo but can't be withdrawn until it drops back under the vanilla limit — so empty your silos first if you plan to uninstall.
[/list]

[b]Optional integrations[/b] (all detected automatically; nothing breaks if they're absent):
[list]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5382]Item Bags[/url] — building from bags, and item totals
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/24842]Cornucopia[/url] — its artisan machines in the price tooltip
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/27049]Wildflour's Atelier Goods[/url] — its machines in the price tooltip
[/list]
