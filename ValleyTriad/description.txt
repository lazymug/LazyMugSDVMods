[size=6][b]🃏 Valley Triad[/b][/size]

[i]A cozy card game for Pelican Town — in the spirit of Triple Triad.[/i]

[color=#e0a030][b]🚧 WORK IN PROGRESS — v0.1.0 (preview) 🚧[/b][/color]

[size=6][b]🇬🇧 English[/b][/size]

[size=5][b]📖 Description[/b][/size]
[b]Valley Triad[/b] brings a Triple Triad–style collectible card game to Stardew Valley.
Challenge the villagers, capture their cards, and build your collection — all rendered in a
warm, pixel-art style. ✨ Every card has its own illustration — a themed scene with the subject
in the spotlight — framed by a card chassis that the mod draws procedurally at runtime.

[color=#c88]ℹ️ [b]AI disclosure:[/b] the 52 card illustrations were produced with the help of
AI image generation and are bundled with the mod. The card frames, coins, name plate and the
fallback scenes are drawn by code. No Stardew Valley assets are redistributed.[/color]

[size=5][b]⭐ Main features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]🎴 [b]Full Triple Triad match[/b] — 3×3 board, five cards each, four edge values per card
(1–10, where 10 shows as "A"). [b]Same, Plus and Combo[/b] rules for big chain captures.
[*]🌱 [b]Elemental tiles[/b] — a card matching a tile's season gets +1 to all edges (−1 on a mismatch).
[*]⚔️ [b]Sudden death[/b] — ties are replayed with the cards you currently control.
[*]📚 [b]52-card collection[/b] across four rarities — crops, forageables, farm animals, fish,
monsters, minerals, villagers, and legendary bosses (the Wizard, the Junimo King, Mr. Qi,
the Crimsonfish, the Prismatic Shard).
[*]🛠️ [b]Deck builder[/b] — pick 5 cards (max 1 Legendary, 2 Rare).
[*]🍺 [b]Meet Abigail at the Saloon[/b] — she teaches you the game and hands over a starter deck,
then a guided tutorial match.
[*]🗓️ [b]Challenge villagers on Fridays[/b] at the Saloon — walk up with an empty hand and press
the action button. Win to claim one of their cards and gain friendship. 💛
[*]🎲 [b]Stakes, your call[/b] — [b]Friendly[/b] (win cards, never lose one), [b]Hard[/b] (lose a
random card you played), or [b]Ragnarök[/b] (winner takes your most valuable card).
[*]🎨 [b]Illustrated cards[/b] — 52 hand-composed illustrations (AI-assisted, see the note above),
set in a procedurally drawn frame whose coins, name plate and rarity gems are generated by code.
[*]🌐 [b]Localization[/b] — English and Português (pt-BR).
[/list]

peixes, monstros, minerais, moradores e chefes lendários (o Bruxo, o Rei Junimo, o Sr. Qi,

[*]🛠️ [b]Montador de deck[/b] — escolha 5 cartas (máx. 1 Lendária, 2 Raras).
[*]🍺 [b]Encontre a Abigail no Saloon[/b] — ela te ensina o jogo, te dá um deck inicial e depois
uma partida-tutorial guiada.
[*]🗓️ [b]Desafie os moradores às sextas[/b] no Saloon — chegue perto com a mão vazia e aperte o
botão de ação. Ao vencer, escolha uma carta do adversário e ganhe amizade. 💛
[*]🎲 [b]As apostas são sua escolha[/b] — [b]Amigável[/b] (ganha cartas, nunca perde),
[b]Difícil[/b] (perde uma carta aleatória que jogou) ou [b]Ragnarök[/b] (o vencedor leva a sua
carta mais valiosa).
[*]🎨 [b]Cartas ilustradas[/b] — 52 ilustrações próprias (com auxílio de IA, veja o aviso acima),
